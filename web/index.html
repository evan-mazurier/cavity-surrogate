Inside the training range the surrogate matches the solver to about 1e-6 — the solver's own convergence floor. Outside it the error climbs immediately: the model interpolates a validated solver, it does not contain the physics.

What this is

Square box of fluid, top wall sliding to the right. 86 runs of a projection-method solver (validated against Ghia et al. 1982, see the repo) were reduced to 20 spatial modes; each mode's weight is a cubic spline in log Re. Every field here is mean + Σ aₖ(Re) φₖ, computed in this page.
